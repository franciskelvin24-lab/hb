Official Policies & Service Terms

Welcome to HomeBasket. These policies explain the standards, responsibilities and procedures governing our sourcing, ordering, payment, supply and delivery services. They are intended to promote transparency, protect customers and business partners, and ensure that every transaction is handled responsibly.

By placing an order, supplying products or undertaking a delivery assignment through HomeBasket, you agree to comply with the terms applicable to your role and transaction.

1. Customer service terms

Ordering

Customers may browse products and submit order requests through HomeBasket's website and approved communication channels. Please provide accurate contact information, delivery address, order details and instructions. An order request is subject to product availability and HomeBasket's confirmation; submitting a request does not by itself guarantee stock or create an accepted order.

For a website order request, follow the instructions shown after checkout and send the prepared request through WhatsApp so our team receives it. HomeBasket will confirm the accepted products and quantities, applicable charges and expected delivery arrangements before proceeding.

Quotations and pricing

Displayed product prices and checkout totals are estimates until confirmed for the specific order. A quotation or order confirmation will identify the relevant products, quantities, prices, delivery charges and any other applicable costs. If a price changes before confirmation, HomeBasket will tell the customer and obtain approval before proceeding at the revised price. No undisclosed charge should be added.

HomeBasket determines the customer-facing selling price. Supplier wholesale prices, internal sourcing costs, margins and other supplier commercial arrangements are confidential and are not part of the customer quotation.

Payment

The website checkout does not collect payment. After confirming an order and its total, HomeBasket will communicate the payment method and instructions applicable to that transaction. Customers must be told the amount due before making payment and should use only payment instructions confirmed by HomeBasket.

An order requiring advance payment is not treated as paid or ready to proceed until payment has been verified. A payment message, screenshot or attempted transaction is not, on its own, confirmation that funds have been received. Customers should report failed or duplicate transactions promptly. Any refund due will be reviewed against the order and verified payment and handled through an appropriate payment route, subject to provider procedures and applicable law. HomeBasket will communicate the outcome and any relevant next steps.

Product quality, inspection and safety

HomeBasket takes reasonable steps to source products from reliable suppliers. Products supplied should correspond with the descriptions, quantities and specifications confirmed for the order. Where practicable, visible damage, contamination, incorrect quantities and other obvious issues should be checked before dispatch. Food and other sensitive products should be handled with appropriate care.

Where applicable, expiry or best-before dates should be checked before supply. Expired products must not knowingly be supplied, and products should have a reasonable remaining shelf life for their intended use. Relevant product information or limitations will be communicated before purchase where necessary. Customers should report quality or safety concerns promptly.

Product substitutions

HomeBasket may offer an alternative if a requested item is unavailable. No materially different product will be substituted without the customer's approval. The customer may accept or reject a proposed substitute; any price difference must be disclosed before approval, and a rejected substitute will not be charged as an accepted item.

Missing, damaged or incorrect items

Please inspect your delivery as soon as reasonably possible. Report missing, damaged, incorrect or defective items through the contact channel below, quoting your order reference and including supporting photographs where appropriate. HomeBasket will review the report and determine an appropriate resolution, which may include replacement, correction, refund or another reasonable remedy depending on the circumstances. These terms do not limit consumer rights available under Nigerian law.

Order changes and cancellations

Request changes or cancellation as early as possible. A request made after sourcing, purchase or dispatch has started may not be fully accommodated. Where lawful and properly disclosed, reasonable costs already incurred may apply. HomeBasket will communicate whether a change or cancellation can be accommodated and how any resulting refund will be handled.

Delivery

Delivery arrangements, charges and estimated delivery times will be communicated before order completion. An estimate is not a guarantee unless expressly confirmed. Customers must provide a valid delivery address and reachable contact details and make reasonable arrangements to receive the order. HomeBasket will communicate material delays, failed delivery attempts and proposed changes when the relevant information is available. Any additional delivery charge must be disclosed and approved before it is incurred.

Complaints and refunds

Customers may raise complaints using HomeBasket's approved contact channels. Please include the order reference and relevant details so the matter can be reviewed. HomeBasket will acknowledge and review complaints within a reasonable period and communicate the outcome. A refund, where due, will be processed through an appropriate payment method subject to payment-provider procedures and verification. No unreasonable condition in these terms is intended to deny a valid complaint or a right provided by law.

2. Supplier terms and conditions

  • Approval and verification: Supplier onboarding is subject to HomeBasket's review and any checks appropriate to the products and service. Applying or being listed does not guarantee approval, selection or an order.
  • Accurate information: Suppliers must provide accurate product descriptions, specifications, quantities, availability, expiry information where relevant, quotations and lead times. Notify HomeBasket promptly of changes, shortages or unavailable products.
  • Quality and safety: Supplied goods must meet applicable product safety and quality requirements and the specifications agreed for the order. Expiry dates must be checked where relevant; expired products must not knowingly be supplied.
  • Quotations and orders: Supplier quotations should state the products, quantities, supply prices, availability and fulfilment terms. A supplier must not change an agreed supply price or material term without notifying HomeBasket and obtaining agreement before fulfilment.
  • Fulfilment and handling: Accepted orders must be prepared within the agreed timeframe, accurately counted and appropriately packaged and handled. Report delays, shortages, damage or errors as soon as possible and cooperate on a practical resolution.
  • Confidentiality: Supplier wholesale prices, HomeBasket sourcing information, customer information and other non-public business information must be kept confidential and used only for the relevant business purpose. Supplier wholesale prices and internal supplier information are not for public display.
  • Conduct and settlement: Suppliers must communicate professionally and cooperate with reasonable order checks. Payment and settlement follow the terms agreed for the relevant supplier relationship or transaction.
  • Suspension or termination: HomeBasket may pause requests or end a supplier relationship for serious or repeated breaches, materially inaccurate information, safety concerns or failure to meet agreed fulfilment terms. This does not remove any rights or obligations arising from an existing transaction.

3. Delivery partner terms

  • Handle assigned orders responsibly and follow the agreed delivery instructions and reasonable safety requirements.
  • Protect products against avoidable damage, contamination, tampering or loss during collection and transport.
  • Treat customers respectfully and keep names, phone numbers, addresses, order details and delivery instructions confidential. Use these details only to complete the assigned delivery.
  • Do not demand or collect an undisclosed charge from a customer. Refer questions about charges to HomeBasket.
  • Accurately report collection, delivery completion, failed delivery attempts, delays and delivery-related incidents through the agreed channel.
  • Promptly report lost, damaged, tampered-with or undelivered orders and cooperate with HomeBasket to review and resolve the incident.
  • HomeBasket may pause future assignments or end a delivery arrangement for serious or repeated failure to meet these terms, subject to any existing agreement.

4. HomeBasket operating standards

In coordinating sourcing, supply and delivery, HomeBasket will take reasonable steps to:

  • Source products through suppliers considered suitable for the relevant goods and order.
  • Review product availability and supplier quotations and communicate confirmed customer-facing prices and charges before proceeding.
  • Coordinate supplier fulfilment and, where practicable, check order contents before dispatch.
  • Coordinate delivery arrangements and communicate relevant order updates when information is available.
  • Keep appropriate transaction and complaint records for service coordination and follow-up.
  • Handle customer, supplier and delivery partner information responsibly and share only information relevant to the transaction with the parties who need it.
  • Review operational errors and service complaints and communicate a reasonable response or proposed resolution.

These standards are applied according to the nature of the order, available information and circumstances. They do not guarantee that every product or delivery will be available at a particular time.

5. Privacy and information handling

HomeBasket uses contact, order and delivery information to respond to requests, confirm orders, coordinate supply and delivery, handle complaints and maintain relevant transaction records. Information may be shared with a supplier or delivery partner only to the extent needed to fulfil or resolve the relevant transaction.

Customers, suppliers and delivery partners should provide information relevant to their transaction and avoid sending passwords, full payment-card details or other sensitive credentials through messaging channels. HomeBasket will handle personal information in accordance with applicable Nigerian data-protection requirements and use reasonable care to protect it against unauthorised disclosure or misuse.

  • Availability and market changes: Product availability and supplier prices may change. HomeBasket will communicate material changes and seek approval for a revised customer price before proceeding.
  • Events outside reasonable control: Delays or interruptions may arise from events beyond reasonable operational control, including severe weather, transport disruption, public emergencies or supplier interruption. HomeBasket will take reasonable steps to communicate material effects and discuss available options.

  • Privacy: Personal information will be handled for legitimate service and transaction purposes, in accordance with applicable Nigerian law.
  • Policy updates: HomeBasket may update these policies as its services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page. Changes apply to new transactions from publication; an existing transaction remains subject to the terms confirmed for it unless the parties agree otherwise or the law requires otherwise.
  • Applicable law: These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, including applicable consumer-protection and data-protection requirements. Nothing in these terms excludes a legal right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.
